# Decision Trail – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Decision Trail is a Forge app for Atlassian Jira and Rovo, built by Gokhan Atalay (Neqro).

## What data the app accesses
- Jira work items, linked work items and their comments, to find decisions.
- Atlassian account IDs and display names of comment authors, and their teams and goals from Teamwork Graph, to show who decided and who to ask.

## What data the app stores
- Decisions you choose to record (statement, rationale, evidence links, the Atlassian account ID of who recorded it and when), stored per work item in Forge storage.
- A cache of a user's team names keyed by Atlassian account ID, in Forge storage, so the answer still shows teams if Teamwork Graph is briefly unavailable.

All data stays on Atlassian's Forge platform. The app has no external servers, sends no data outside Atlassian, uses no third-party services and does not sell or share data.

## Retention and deletion
Recorded decisions can be removed at any time with the Remove button in the Jira panel. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its Forge storage according to Atlassian's Forge data retention rules.
